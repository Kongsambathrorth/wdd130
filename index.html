Cambodia

Phnom Penh. The capital city of Cambodia, Phnom Penh, is located in the southern-central region of the nation. It is located at the point where three rivers meet, including the Bassac, Tonle Sab, and Mekong rivers. Founded in 1434, Phnom Penh carries an abundant history of significant past events to current peace.
